How it works

Three steps. You stay Captain.

1

Connect your CV once

Upload your master resume + cover letter. Pick your target roles, salary floor, and the cities you'd work in. Five minutes, then never again.

2

Daily fits in your dashboard

Every morning, 50-100 fresh jobs ranked by fit. Click any card to see the full description, the JD tailored to your profile, and your rendered CV + cover letter side-by-side.

3

Approve. We submit. You get hired.

Hit Approve and our browser agent fills the form, uploads your materials, and pauses for your final click. You stay in control of every submission.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will my applications look spammy? ↓

No. Each application is generated from your master CV + the job description, tailored for that specific role. The bot never sends template-spam. Every form is paused at the Submit button for your final click.

What ATS systems do you support? ↓

Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Personio, and Workday have full integration. For everything else, our browser agent fills the form, uploads your materials, and pauses for your click. We're adding new adapters weekly.

Can I cancel anytime? ↓

Yes. One click in the customer portal, no questions asked. You'll keep access until the end of the billing period, then drop back to the Free tier.

Is my data safe? GDPR? ↓

Yes. EU-Frankfurt data residency, encrypted at rest, full account deletion within 24 hours of request. We're GDPR-compliant and ship a Data Processing Agreement on request.
